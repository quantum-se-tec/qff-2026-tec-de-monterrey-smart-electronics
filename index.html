About the event

Learn quantum computing, build with Qiskit, and put your skills into practice.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 @ Tecnológico de Monterrey – Smart Electronics is an in-person learning experience that combines guided theory, practical examples, and hands-on work with Qiskit.

Participants will start with the foundations of quantum computing and an introduction to Qiskit from the first session. Over the following days, they will deepen their understanding of quantum circuits, simulation, and practical workflows before moving into a team-based hackathon.

The goal is to help participants understand why the concepts matter, become comfortable using Qiskit, and apply what they learn to a solution they can explain and present.

Growing the quantum community

Quantum computing is an emerging field with potential applications across science, engineering, industry, and society. Hackathon

Use Qiskit to turn what you learned into a working solution.

Participants will work in teams on a hackathon challenge and develop a solution with Qiskit that can be submitted and evaluated. The hackathon connects the workshop content with a practical problem-solving experience.

Teams will begin working on Friday and will have additional time to develop, test, document, and prepare their solutions before the Saturday showcase.

Challenge options, team format, submission requirements, judging criteria, and awards will be announced once confirmed.

Form a team→ Develop with Qiskit→ Test & document→ Present

Saturday collaboration

A shared space with the Quantum and Technology Symposium.

The final showcase is planned in collaboration with the Quantum and Technology Symposium, an event led by the Quantum Computing Club (QCC), with the IEEE Student Branch as a principal collaborator and support from other members of the organizing community.

The Symposium program is planned to include invited speakers and student research poster presentations, with a strong emphasis on quantum and other emerging technologies. Fall Fest teams will have dedicated space during the shared Saturday program to present their hackathon solutions.

Qiskit Fall Fest and the Quantum and Technology Symposium remain separate events while sharing part of the Saturday experience. Additional information about Symposium access and activities will be published once confirmed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous quantum computing experience?

No. The workshop starts from the fundamentals.

Do I need previous Qiskit experience?

No. Qiskit will be introduced from the first workshop session.

Do I need programming experience?

Basic programming knowledge is recommended, but no specific programming language requirement has been finalized.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, notebook, pens or pencils, and your Tecnológico de Monterrey student ID.

Is there a registration fee?

No. Participation is free.

Will the event be in English or Spanish?

The main workshop is expected to be delivered in Spanish. Some activities, including the IBM Quantum guest session, will be delivered in English.

Do I need an IBM Quantum account?

This is still being finalized. Technical requirements will be shared with registered participants before the event.

Can students from another Tecnológico de Monterrey campus participate?

Yes, provided they can attend the event in person at Campus Monterrey.
